
# Bright-Coffee-Shop-Case-Study

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a coffee shop sales dataset to uncover insights into **sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, and sales trends**.

The main goal was to understand which products generate the most revenue, identify sales patterns across different products and time periods, and provide recommendations that could help improve the coffee shop's overall sales performance.

This project demonstrates how data analysis can transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support better decision-making.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Identify the products generating the most revenue.
* Determine which products perform the best based on sales.
* Analyze sales trends across different products and time periods.
* Identify patterns in customer purchasing behaviour.
* Provide recommendations for improving sales performance.

## Dataset

The dataset contains coffee shop transaction and sales information. It covers the period from **1 January to 19 May 2023**. The data was analyzed to understand product performance, revenue, sales volume, and purchasing patterns.

## Summary of Insights

The analysis showed that **overall sales improved during the period**, with revenue increasing from February to April and average daily revenue continuing to grow into May.

**Coffee and Tea were the strongest categories**, together making up most of the shop's revenue and units sold. This shows that these categories play an important role in the shop's overall performance.

**Barista Espresso was the strongest product type**, while the top products overall generated most of the shop's revenue.

Sales were strongest during the **morning period**, especially between around 08:00 and 10:30. Mid-morning was also the strongest time period for sales.

Customer purchases were generally small, with most transactions containing only **one or two items**. This suggests that customers usually make simple purchases rather than buying many items at once.

The analysis also showed that **selling more units does not always mean generating more revenue**. Some products generate more revenue per unit than others, meaning product value is also important when looking at performance.

Sales became much lower after the morning period and remained relatively flat later in the day. Night-time sales made up only a small part of total revenue.

The three stores performed relatively similarly overall. **Hell's Kitchen consistently recorded the strongest performance**, while **Lower Manhattan had the fewest transactions**.

Overall, the analysis shows that the coffee shop's strongest opportunities are around **popular products, morning sales, and improving sales during quieter periods**.

## Key Insights

The analysis provides an understanding of which products contribute most to revenue, how sales change over time, and what purchasing patterns can be identified from the data.

These insights can help the coffee shop make better decisions around product promotion, inventory management, and sales strategies.
